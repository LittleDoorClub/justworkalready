The demo where the dolphin becomes a keyboard and types on YOUR unlocked PC. Harmless payload (a poem), maximum lesson: an unlocked PC obeys any USB that plugs in.

  1. Gabe: unlock the PC, sit on the desktop. Badger: opens BadKB → Demos, eyes on the LCD confirm the ghost_poet bar.
  2. Badger: RIGHT → Run → OK. Al flips USB to HID — COM3 dies, that's normal, hands off 5 seconds (v2 driver-settle delay).
  3. Notepad opens itself; the ghost poem types letter by letter. Device Manager even shows the new "HID Keyboard Device" mid-typing.
  4. The lesson, out loud: v1 failed because the driver install ate the keystrokes — real attackers pad delays for exactly this. Forensics (RunMRU registry) proved the miss. Receipts over vibes, even for ghosts.
  5. The defense: Win+L is the whole shield. A locked screen turns a ghost keyboard into a poet with no page. Mystery keyboard in Device Manager = ghost at the port.
